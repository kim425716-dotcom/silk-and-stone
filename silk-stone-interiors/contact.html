What Sets Us Apart

The SILK & STONE difference

Personal Attention

Every project is personally led by Simon Mwakodi and our senior design-build leads — who are with you from the first conversation to the final reveal. No junior handoffs.

Material Excellence

Since our establishment in 2022, we have built trusted relationships with exceptional material suppliers, stone carvers, and master craftspeople across Kenya and Europe. We give you access to bespoke quality that simply isn't available off the shelf.

End-to-End Management

We handle every detail of your project — from ordering and logistics to contractor coordination and final installation — so you can enjoy the process rather than manage it.

Transparent Partnership

We believe in complete transparency on costs, timelines, and every decision point. Our shared project management system keeps you informed and in control at every stage.
