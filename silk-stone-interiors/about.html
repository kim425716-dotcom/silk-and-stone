Our Story

A studio built on curiosity, craft, and conviction

SILK & STONE began with a clear conviction: that interior architecture and bespoke design at its highest expression should feel personal, material, and alive. We spent years working to bring uncompromising craft, tactile richness, and turnkey design-build excellence to East Africa's most discerning clients.

Today, with a curated team of dedicated designers, architects, and project managers who share the same founding belief: that the spaces we inhabit shape how we feel, think, and connect. Every project is a careful dialogue between client and designer — your story becomes the foundation for something truly extraordinary.

From intimate Nairobi apartments and sprawling Karen estates to celebrated commercial environments across East Africa, we bring the same rigour, empathy, and aesthetic intelligence to every commission regardless of scale.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

L

Listen First

Every great space starts with understanding. We immerse ourselves in your life, your daily rhythms, your personal aspirations, and even the things you cannot yet articulate. We believe deeply that the client is always the best brief.

M

Material Truth

We honour natural materials — stone, hardwood, linen, hand-thrown clay, aged brass, and raw plaster — letting their inherent texture, weight, and beauty guide the design. Materials chosen with conviction age beautifully. Trend-chasing never does.

T

Timeless Craft

Trends fade with the seasons. We design for the decades ahead, creating harmonious spaces that age gracefully and grow richer with time. Our projects are built with the kind of careful attention to joinery, proportion, and finish that separates design from decoration.

E

Emotional Resonance

A room should do more than look beautiful in a photograph. It should make you feel something the moment you enter — calm, inspired, held. We design not just for the eye, but for the body and spirit that inhabit the space every day.

P

Precision in Practice

Beautiful design is only as good as its execution. We are rigorous project managers as much as designers — coordinating contractors, artisans, and suppliers with the same precision we bring to a concept board. The details are where the difference lives.

S

Sustainable Intention

We favour local craftsmanship, sustainable sourcing, and materials with low environmental impact wherever possible. Longevity is itself a form of sustainability — a well-made space outlasts ten poorly made ones.

A word from our principal

"I started SILK & STONE with one belief: that the way we design our homes and workplaces is a direct expression of how we value life itself. To design well is an act of care — for the person who will live there, for the craftspeople who build it, and for the world that provides the materials."
Simon Mwakodi Architect & Lead Designer · SILK & STONE

Our Journey

Our trajectory of craft, intention, and growth

2022

Studio Established

SILK & STONE is established in Nairobi with a focused commitment to natural materiality, quiet luxury, and bespoke design-build architecture.

Atelier & Custom Furniture Launch
